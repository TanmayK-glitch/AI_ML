### Intuition
- The core Intuition of a LR is to draw a Best Fit Line across the scattered DS, this line is considered best as it makes the (Minimum Error) when passing through the scattered points.

- Mathematical Intuition: it used eq: y = mx + b where it figures out the value of m(slope) and b(intercept/offset). 

- The slope m represents the 'weightage of dependency'. This tells how much the result is actually dependent on the 'b'. If m is high then dependecy is high and vice-versa. The slope b represents the 'offset' or 'baseline', even if the value of b is ZERO the model still gives the output. Ex if YOE is 0 it dosent mean the person is gonna get 0LPA (Package = m * YOE) here the person will still get a Base Package.

- m is the slope of the line, it is a coefficient that gets multiplied with the CGPA or YOE and it tell us the, predicted package changes for every one unit of CGPA or YOE change.
